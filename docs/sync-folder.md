# Sync Folder

Sync Folder keeps files in your Home folder's `Sync` folder in sync between two
computers. Follow these steps on both computers to connect them and share the
folder.

## Step 1: Turn it on

On both computers:

1. Click the top-right corner of your screen, where the battery, Wi-Fi, and
   volume icons are.
2. Click **Sync Folder** so it turns blue. This creates a folder named `Sync` in
   your Home folder.

## Step 2: Connect the two computers

1. On both computers, click the arrow next to **Sync Folder** in that same
   top-right menu, then click **Open Web GUI** to open the sharing page in your
   browser.
2. On Computer 1, click **Actions → Show ID** in the top right of the page.
   Leave that window open.
3. On Computer 2, click **Add Remote Device** at the bottom right of the sharing
   page, then paste or type Computer 1's ID and click **Save**.
4. On Computer 1, a yellow banner will ask if you want to connect. Click
   **Add Device**, then **Save**.

## Step 3: Share the folder

1. On either computer, find the **Sync Folder** box on the sharing page.
2. Click **Edit**, then the **Sharing** tab.
3. Check the box next to your other computer's name and click **Save**.
4. On the other computer, accept the prompt to share the folder and click
   **Save**.

Drop a file into the `Sync` folder on one computer. It will appear in the `Sync`
folder on the other computer automatically.

For more options, see the [Syncthing getting started guide](https://docs.syncthing.net/intro/getting-started.html).
Syncthing is the application that synchronizes the files.
