Bluefin was fortunate to get nominated for a "Ship and Tell" session at GitHub Universe.

[![Watch: What open source can teach us about agents in CI/CD](/img/blog/2026-10-30-github-universe-2026-trip-report/ship-and-tell.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bUhCBYi4dys)

A little while ago [we had a thread](https://github.com/ublue-os/bluefin/discussions/4949) in our discussions about the nightmare that is printing on Linux. What started as a joke turned into an actual thing that we built. It was such a cool story that Martin Woodward mentioned us in his closing keynote at Universe!

[![Watch: GitHub Universe 2026 closing keynote, starting at 15:43](/img/blog/2026-10-30-github-universe-2026-trip-report/closing-keynote.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gh-hg7lwA0o&t=943s)

I've gotten feedback in the Discord from multiple people who have these working. We could really use more data/feedback/reports from those of you who have printers that need these drivers.

It was also a great event to spend time with Kyle Gospodnetich (co-creator of Bazzite). This gave us an opportunity to catch up on a bunch of things. Bazzite's build system just got reworked into a Python for a super clean design. It was exciting to hear Kyle talk about the new people that are coming on board. Just a week prior I had spent time with [Cameron Knauff](https://www.youtube.com/@PizzaLovingNerd), the maintainer of StillOS. Same enery, the cloud native experts are starting to show up in larger numbers and everyone is accelerating.

We had a great 30+ minute discussion with Godot developers. Kyle spent a good deal of time explaining the Flatpak process to them, you love to see it.

We had some great sushi too:

![Sushi dinner](/img/blog/2026-10-30-github-universe-2026-trip-report/sushi.png)

Overall we had a great show, and it was awesome. I was inspired by the Home Assistant and AutoGPT developers as well, we have lots of experts to learn from! This was a bucket list event for me, we had to get nominated before even being submitted, it's very hard to get a talk here so it was great to be able to present your work in front of these developers!

A huge shoutout to GitHub for their direct support to Bluefin,
[it's not the first time](https://docs.projectbluefin.io/blog/2025/08/29/github-oss-fund/)!
