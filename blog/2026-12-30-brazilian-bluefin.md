## Intro

Hi there, I'm Delphic Melody. I'm the vector artist who's been creating vector art for Project Bluefin. You might be familiar with some of the vector wallpapers that I've created by remastering and expanding on the work of the extremely talented Natalia Jagielska and Atius Amy: [Wallpapers](https://ko-fi.com/album/Bluefin-F1F61U20BN)
